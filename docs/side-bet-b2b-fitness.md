# Side bet — B2B fitness tooling (discovery probe)

A parallel, low-cost probe running alongside the Roblox bet during the "test the water" phase. Not a repo yet, this is discovery only.

## The thesis
- The user is a fitness-industry insider. mfit failed as a *consumer* tracker because domain knowledge doesn't differentiate a consumer app. The B2B angle is where insider knowledge actually converts: trainers/gyms drowning in client admin, a market gap the research confirms is underserved.
- Targeting the **Australian** market (Melbourne trainers/gyms/studios) deliberately, local customers are the only side-business revenue that also builds toward staying in Australia, and they're reachable.

## The method (customer discovery, Mom Test style)
- Do NOT pitch an app. Ask about their actual week and real pains. The moment you say "app," you contaminate the data.
- Outreach: warm, short IG DMs. As of **2026-07-12**, 5 messages sent to Melbourne trainers. Waiting on replies (async, hence running Roblox in parallel).
- Template used:
  > Hey [name], stumbled on your page in [where]. I'm Ten, I love fitness and building software. What's the most annoying or time-sucking part of client admin for you right now? Just trying to understand how trainers spend their time. Not selling you anything.
- **The key follow-up when they reply**: "how do you handle that now?" That reveals whether it's a real pay-to-fix problem (they've cobbled together spreadsheets / multiple apps) or a minor gripe.
- Listen for: the **same specific pain from two different people, unprompted.** That is the product signal.

## The trigger (when this wins the side-business slot)
If two trainers describe the same pain and one asks to see a solution → fitness B2B wins and Roblox parks. Decide this while calm, act on it when it fires.
